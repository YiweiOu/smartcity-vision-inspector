The image shows a fire alarm control panel installed on a concrete wall. The panel is labeled as a 'JB-BG-SCM5型火灾报警控制器（联动型）', indicating it is a centralized fire alarm system with interlocking capabilities. ## Findings

### Finding 1 | Unsecured documentation · severity: **low**

- **Evidence**: Two sheets of paper are taped directly to the wall adjacent to the fire alarm control panel.
- **Why it matters**: Paper documents attached near the fire alarm control panel can become dislodged during emergencies or routine maintenance, potentially obstructing access or causing confusion. Additionally, if these papers are not properly secured, they may interfere with the operation of the panel or create a tripping hazard if they fall. While not immediately dangerous, this represents poor housekeeping and could compromise emergency response efficiency.
- **Cited clause**: Fire Protection Law of the PRC (《中华人民共和国消防法》), Art. 28 — Evacuation routes and exits must be kept clear at all times; occupying, blocking or sealing them is strictly prohibited.

### Finding 2 | Lack of protective signage · severity: **medium**

- **Evidence**: No warning sign or identification label is visible on or near the fire alarm control panel.
- **Why it matters**: Fire alarm control panels are critical safety devices that require clear identification and protection from unauthorized tampering or accidental damage. The absence of a warning sign such as 'Fire Alarm Control Panel — Do Not Tamper' increases the risk of interference by non-qualified personnel. This also violates standard practices for marking essential fire safety equipment.
- **Cited clause**: GB/T 13869 General guide for safety of electric user (《用电安全导则》) — Equipment should be clearly marked and protected from unauthorized access.

### Finding 3 | Potential electrical hazard due to conduit installation · severity: **low**

- **Evidence**: Flexible metal conduit runs above the panel and appears loosely secured with cable ties.
- **Why it matters**: While the conduit itself is properly installed, its loose attachment could lead to mechanical stress on wires over time, especially in environments with vibration or movement. If the conduit becomes detached, it could expose wiring or cause short circuits. Although no immediate danger is evident, long-term risks exist without proper securing.
- **Cited clause**: GB/T 13869 General guide for safety of electric user (《用电安全导则》) — Wiring must be protected by conduit and installed properly.

## Verdict

The scene contains minor fire safety hazards related to documentation placement, lack of signage, and potential electrical conduit issues, but no immediate life-threatening risks are present.

## Remediation Plan

1. Remove or securely fasten any loose papers near the fire alarm control panel to prevent obstruction or interference.
2. Install a clear warning sign (e.g., 'Fire Alarm Control Panel — Authorized Personnel Only') directly on or adjacent to the panel.
3. Inspect and re-secure the flexible conduit above the panel using appropriate clamps or supports to ensure long-term integrity.
