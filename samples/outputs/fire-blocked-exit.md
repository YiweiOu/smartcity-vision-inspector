## Scene

A storage area adjacent to a stainless steel door, with various materials and tools stored on both sides.

## Overall Analysis

The image shows a narrow passageway or storage space flanked by shelves and construction materials. On the left side, long pipes and rods are leaning against the wall, some wrapped in plastic. A red broom or mop is visible near the center-left. To the right, metal shelving holds boxes, bags, and other supplies, including what appears to be electrical components and packaging materials. The central feature is a closed stainless steel door with decorative patterns, likely an exit or access point. The area appears cluttered, with items stored close to the door and along the pathway.

## Findings

### Finding 1 | Blocked evacuation route · severity: **high**

- **Evidence**: Construction materials (pipes, brooms) are stored directly beside and partially obstructing the doorway, reducing clearance.
- **Why it matters**: The presence of stored items near the door impedes quick egress during an emergency. In case of fire, this obstruction could delay evacuation and hinder firefighter access.
- **Cited clause**: Fire Protection Law of the PRC (《中华人民共和国消防法》), Art. 28

### Finding 2 | Obstructed fire service access · severity: **medium**

- **Evidence**: Shelving and stored goods extend into the corridor, narrowing the passage width.
- **Why it matters**: Narrowed pathways reduce the ability of personnel or equipment to move quickly through the area, especially during emergencies. This may also prevent proper deployment of firefighting tools.
- **Cited clause**: Fire Protection Law of the PRC (《中华人民共和国消防法》), Art. 28

### Finding 3 | Improper storage of flammable materials · severity: **medium**

- **Evidence**: Plastic-wrapped materials and cardboard boxes are stored in close proximity to the door and in confined spaces.
- **Why it matters**: Plastic and cardboard are combustible materials; when stored densely in enclosed areas, they can contribute to rapid fire spread if ignited. Lack of ventilation and separation increases risk.
- **Cited clause**: Fire Protection Law of the PRC (《中华人民共和国消防法》), Art. 28

## Verdict

The scene contains multiple fire hazards, primarily due to blocked exits and improper storage of combustible materials.

## Remediation Plan

1. Clear all items from within 1 meter of the door to ensure unobstructed access and egress.
2. Relocate flammable materials such as plastic-wrapped items and cardboard boxes to designated storage areas away from doors and corridors.
3. Ensure that all evacuation routes remain clear at all times and conduct regular inspections.
